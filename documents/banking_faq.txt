
DEMO BANKING POLICY
For educational demonstration only.
These are simulated rules, not real bank policies.

HOME LOAN DOCUMENTS
Applicants generally provide identity proof,
address proof, income proof, recent bank statements,
and relevant property documents. Additional
documents may be required depending on the applicant.

PERSONAL LOAN
A personal loan is an unsecured loan used for
personal expenses. Eligibility, interest rates,
loan amounts, and repayment tenure depend on
the bank's official terms and the applicant's profile.

EQUATED MONTHLY INSTALLMENT (EMI)
EMI is the fixed periodic payment made by a borrower
to repay a loan. It generally includes principal
and interest.

MISSED EMI
Missing an EMI may result in late payment charges,
additional interest, or other consequences according
to the applicable loan agreement and bank policy.

DEBIT CARD BLOCKING
Customers should use the bank's official app,
website, or verified customer support channel to
report a lost or stolen debit card and request blocking.
